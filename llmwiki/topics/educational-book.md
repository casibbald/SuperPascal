## Teaching approach

- 3 × 12-week curriculum (36 weeks total)
- Offline-first deployment (no internet required)
- Hands-on game development as learning vehicle
- Progressive complexity from basics to game engines
- Teacher Guide v1.0 available

## Target audience

- Schools and universities
- STEM education programs
- Self-learners
- Retro computing enthusiasts

## Offline-first design

SuperPascal is designed for environments without internet access:
- Compiler runs locally on Zeal hardware
- Book content included in distribution
- No external dependencies
